实际利率Real Interest Rate：名义利率减通胀预期。油价跌→通胀预期降→实际利率反而升→持有黄金的机会成本增加→金价跌。这就是为什么油价跌黄金也跌。Nominal interest rate minus inflation expectations. Oil falls → inflation expectations drop → real rates rise → the opportunity cost of holding gold increases → gold falls. This is why oil and gold can decline together.
