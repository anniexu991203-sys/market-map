📖 术语白话📖 Plain Language
地缘风险溢价Geopolitical Risk Premium：因为担心打仗，投资者要求额外回报才肯持有资产。一旦风险下降，这部分“恐惧折扣”被去掉，资产价格就跳涨。Extra return investors demand for holding assets when they fear conflict. Once risk falls, this 'fear discount' is removed and prices jump.
和平宣言Peace Declaration：三个核大国联合声明“核战争打不赢也打不得”，并承诺能源稳定供应。等于给市场发了一张“短期内不会发生最坏情况”的保险单。Three nuclear powers jointly declare 'nuclear war cannot be won and must not be fought', promising stable energy supply. Essentially an insurance policy against worst-case scenarios.
风险偏好Risk Appetite：投资者是更愿意买股票（风险资产）还是买国债（避险资产）的倾向。今天明显转向前者。Whether investors prefer stocks (risk assets) or bonds (safe havens). Today clearly shifted to the former.

📖 术语白话📖 Plain Language
鹰派Hawkish：央行官员倾向于加息、收紧货币政策来压制通胀的态度。Kocher说“可能需要进一步加息”就是典型的鹰派信号。Central bank officials' tendency to raise rates and tighten monetary policy to suppress inflation. Kocher saying 'may need further hikes' is a classic hawkish signal.
国债利差Bond Spread：两个国家国债收益率的差额。如果法国债务风险上升，投资者会要求更高的回报才肯买法国国债，法德利差就会走阔——这是欧洲危机的经典预警信号。Yield difference between two countries' government bonds. If France's debt risk rises, investors demand higher returns for French bonds, widening the French-German spread—a classic European crisis warning signal.
终点利率Terminal Rate：市场预期央行加息到哪个水平就会停。如果Kocher暗示还要加，终点利率预期就上移，欧元升值，因为持有欧元资产能获得更高的利息回报。The rate level at which the market expects the central bank to stop hiking. If Kocher hints at more hikes, terminal rate expectations rise and the Euro appreciates, as Euro assets offer higher interest returns.
